Chapter II · Preface

The Editor’s Craft & Calling

Caroline Sonya Renata

Senior Acquisition Editor · Literature & Humanities

F or over twelve years, my days have begun and concluded with the rustle of loose-leaf galley proofs, annotated marginalia, and the exhilarating thrill of opening an unsolicited draft that possesses unmistakable genius. Acquisition is fundamentally an act of quiet faith: looking past raw syntax, structural hesitation, and unfinished codas to perceive the transcendent architecture of an enduring book.

My editorial sanctuary is built upon classical rigor and cultural empathy. I shepherd manuscripts across the delicate bridge that connects an author’s solitary consciousness with the collective memory of the public library. Whether unearthing lyrical fiction from maritime Southeast Asia, commissioning investigative narrative history, or negotiating global subsidiary rights at Frankfurt and London, my compass remains unswerving: clarity, resonance, and prose that demands to be read a generation hence.

Folio Verso p. 42
Critical Takeaways Editorial Marginalia
I.

Splitting digital and translation rights upfront prevents territorial hold-ups and empowers Southeast Asian writers to claim fair royalties globally.

II.

Option clauses must be drafted with precise sunset windows (max 60 days) to prevent authorial paralysis between subsequent manuscripts.

III.

Moral rights (integrity and attribution) must remain fiercely non-negotiable, particularly when licensing into dramatized multimedia.

fact_check Required Proposal Dossier

We accept direct submissions from authors as well as literary representatives. Every formal dossier must contain the following components:

  • check_circle Comprehensive Synopsis: 2 to 3 pages detailing total thematic arc, conclusion, and resolution (no cliffhangers).
  • check_circle Three Sample Chapters: The opening 3 consecutive chapters or initial 50 pages (approx. 10,000–12,000 words).
  • check_circle Authorial Curriculum & Bio: Previous publications, literary awards, academic appointments, and media footprint.
  • check_circle Target Readership Statement: Comparative titles published in the last five years and intended market placement.

Formatting & Reading Timeline

Standard book manuscript format: 12pt Times New Roman or EB Garamond, double spaced, 1-inch margins, formatted as .PDF or .DOCX. Reading turnaround is strictly 4 to 6 weeks. We respond to every formal submission.
